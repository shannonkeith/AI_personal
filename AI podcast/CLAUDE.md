## Project Overview

This is a podcast content repository containing transcripts and summaries from the "AI Daily Brief" podcast. The focus is on enterprise AI adoption, AI-native teams, and strategic technology insights.

**Current Content:**
- `overview.md` - Full episode transcript of the conversation 
- `summary.md` - Structured summary with key insights, organized by topic

## Content Workflows

### Adding New Episodes

When adding new podcast episodes:

1. **Transcript File** - Create `{episode-name}.md` with the full transcript
2. **Summary File** - Create a corresponding `{episode-name}-summary.md` with:
   - Guest name and title
   - Core insight or thesis
   - Key sections with sub-points (use clear hierarchy)
   - Takeaways or action items
   - Referenced products/tools/concepts

Format summaries for scannability - use headers, bullet points, and clear visual hierarchy. Summaries should be 1/5 to 1/10 the length of the full transcript.

### Maintaining Content

- Keep transcripts as the source of truth for exact quotes and speaker details
- Update summaries if new context or corrections are discovered
- Track guest affiliations and titles as they may change over time
- Include timestamps in transcripts if available (currently present in overview.md)
