# AI Daily Brief: Mike Cannon-Brookes on AI-Native Teams - Summary

**Guest:** Mike Cannon-Brookes, CEO of Atlassian  
**Topic:** Building AI-native organizations, enterprise adoption, and moving AI beyond chat

---

## Core Insight

**Intelligence × Context = Business Acceleration**

The key to AI success isn't just better models—it's providing rich organizational context to those models.

---

## Internal AI Adoption at Atlassian

### Challenges
- High talent requirement changes
- Difficult business process reengineering
- Enterprise security & compliance constraints

### Atlassian's Approach
1. **Availability** - Make AI tools accessible to teams (not just 4 out of 13,000)
2. **Culture** - Create skill-sharing loops where employees learn together
3. **Psychological Safety** - Celebrate both wins and failures
4. **Deployment at Scale** - Handle security requirements while moving fast

---

## Rovo Platform: Enterprise AI Infrastructure

The central pillar for all AI capabilities across Atlassian products.

### Key Features
- **Security & Compliance** - Data residency, private model choice, customer-managed keys, granular controls
- **Integration** - Works with existing workflows (JIRA, Confluence, etc.)
- **Enterprise Controls** - URL allowlists/blocklists, memory controls, compliance frameworks

### Customer Reality
- Some take 6 months to evaluate and turn on
- Others adopt in a day
- All require trust verification before use

---

## Teamwork Graph: The Context Layer

**Scale:** 150+ billion objects and connections across enterprise organizations

### Recent Expansions
1. **Code Context**
   - Full semantic index of codebases
   - Helps coding agents understand business & technical context
   - Reduces token usage and inference cost

2. **People Context**
   - Organizational charts
   - Skills tracking and AI adoption measurement
   - Collaboration patterns

3. **Physical Assets**
   - ~50% of customers manage physical assets (equipment, vehicles, etc.)
   - Critical for manufacturing, logistics, field operations

### Access Methods
- **TWG CLI** - 60-70 command sets built for agents
- **MCP Server** - Brings context to any agent framework
- **Browser Integration** (DIA) - Context accessible while browsing

---

## Product Strategy: Dual Approach

### 1. Enhance Existing Workflows
- Text rewriting and creation in Confluence
- Slide generation from documents
- JIRA agent integration into current processes
- **Benefit:** Minimal organizational change, immediate value

### 2. Enable New Ways of Working
- Rovo for filing service requests without forms
- Agent-assisted workflows
- Headless tool use for seamless collaboration
- **Benefit:** Future-proofing, competitive advantage

**Both must happen simultaneously** for organizations to move from AI novice to AI native.

---

## Characteristics of Leading Organizations

### What Sets Them Apart
- **Ambition** - Target 20-30% improvements, not 2%
- **Platform Thinking** - Build integrated constructs, not scattered tools
- **Collaboration** - Ask "how does this connect to our 10 key systems?"
- **Cost Awareness** - Understand token/resource usage (not yet managing, but aware)
- **Outcome Focus** - Measure true productivity metrics

### Wrong Metrics (What Leaders Avoid)
- ❌ Quantity of tokens used
- ❌ Raw output volume
- ✅ Quality of output
- ✅ Throughput and flow
- ✅ Whether tools actually accelerate jobs

---

## Robo Studio & Disposable Software

### The Spectrum
- **Personal Apps** - Non-engineers using Robo Studio, DIA, etc. to solve immediate problems
  - Don't commit to Git
  - No 2 AM wake-ups
  - Meant to be thrown away
  
- **Enterprise Agents** - Full-fledged agentic systems
  - Proper versioning
  - Development teams manage them
  - Long-term stability required

### Security Model
- Personal applications run "as yourself"
- Access only content user already has permission for
- Leverage Teamwork Graph + Atlassian security levels
- Enterprise controls throughout

---

## Future Direction: AI Beyond Chat

### The Shift
From: Chat windows + terminal clients for power users  
To: AI integrated into natural product experiences

### What Excites Leaders
- **Design Era of AI** - Accessible to everyday users
- **Meeting Users Where They Work** - Not forcing people to a chat interface
- **Seamless Integration** - AI as a feature, not a separate tool
- **Across Platforms** - DIA, JIRA, Confluence, Loom, etc.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Context is infrastructure** - Invest in organizational knowledge graphs
2. **Security enables adoption** - Enterprise controls = customer trust = faster deployment
3. **Two-track strategy** - Improve today's workflows AND enable tomorrow's
4. **Measurement matters** - Track outcomes, not activity (tokens, usage)
5. **Culture drives success** - Skill-sharing and psychological safety compound adoption
6. **Agentic tools need platforms** - Not scattered frameworks, integrated ecosystems
7. **The future is integrated** - AI moves from chat to every product experience
