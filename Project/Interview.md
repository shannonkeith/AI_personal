# Pre-Project Interview

## Project Goal & Vision

- What is the primary goal of this project?
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- Who is the end user or audience?
- What is the desired outcome or deliverable?
- How does this project fit into your larger vision or strategy?

## Constraints & Limitations

- What is the timeline for this project?
- What budget or resource constraints exist?
- Are there technical limitations or dependencies?
- What stakeholders or team members are involved?
- Are there any compliance, security, or accessibility requirements?
- What platforms or tools must (or must not) be used?

## Success Criteria

- How will you measure success?
- What does a successful outcome look like?
- What are the key metrics or deliverables?
- What would constitute failure or an unacceptable outcome?
- Are there any must-have features vs. nice-to-have features?

## Current Knowledge & Starting Point

- What do you already know about the topic or technology?
- What experience or expertise do you bring to this project?
- What have you already researched or explored?
- What tools, frameworks, or methodologies are you familiar with?
- Are there any existing resources, code, or documentation to build upon?
- What is your biggest concern or uncertainty about this project?

## Additional Context

- What is the timeline for different phases?
- Are there dependencies on other projects or teams?
- What decisions have already been made?
- What flexibility do you have in scope, timeline, or approach?
